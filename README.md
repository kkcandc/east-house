# East House

A small 3D game set in East Nashville. Walk around inside a house and place the furniture. After three pieces, Nico from Five Points walks in. Punch, kick, or block. Spacing and timing matter: a kick reaches farther, a punch is quicker, and his windup is the moment to counter or cover up.

Win, and that house stays furnished. Walk outside and bloop sidewalk and street pieces onto the ground where you are standing, then choose the next house and decorate it the same way. Each win adds another house and more of the block.

Lose, and only the house you were decorating is cleared. You start that room over. Houses you already kept, and the street pieces, stay put.

The block can include Porter Brick, Shelby Shotgun, Gallatin Skinny, a Five Points corner, Shelby Park, a mural wall, sidewalk bikes, and Tabla Rasa Toy Store.

## Play

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```

Open the local URL. The game is a phone-width column. Drag the picture to look, or click it to capture the mouse. Hold Walk, or drag the gold stick. Place here sets a piece down in front of you. Outside, stand on the sidewalk for a sidewalk piece, or in the road for a street piece, and it bloops onto that ground. In the fight, Punch, Kick, and Block are always there. Step in, step back, and swing when he chambers or just after he misses.
